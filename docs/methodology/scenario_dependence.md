# Scenario dependence analysis — v2

## Why this matters

A single overall ranking can hide the fact that different data-centre strategies value different infrastructure. This analysis therefore treats the four scenarios as deliberately different decision lenses and measures how much their rankings agree.

## Main findings

- The four municipality rankings remain positively related, but they are not interchangeable.
- Hyperscale AI is most similar to the Low-Water AI proxy and Renewable-First Compute.
- Colocation / Digital Hub diverges most from Renewable-First Compute and the Low-Water AI proxy because carrier diversity and digital-hub characteristics carry much more weight.
- The top-50 overlap between scenario pairs ranges from 27 to 39 municipalities, confirming that the scenario design materially changes the candidate universe.

## Specialist examples

Some municipalities become highly attractive only under a particular strategy:
- Santana do Matos (RN) reaches #12 in Renewable-First Compute but falls to #273 in Colocation.
- Jandaíra (RN) reaches #14 Renewable-First but #218 Colocation.
- Cabo de Santo Agostinho (PE) reaches #3 Colocation but #94 Renewable-First.
- Parnaíba (PI) reaches #6 Colocation but #81 in the current Low-Water proxy.
- Morro do Chapéu (BA) reaches #9 Low-Water proxy but #83 Colocation.

This is desirable behaviour: the model is revealing strategic trade-offs rather than forcing every municipality into one universal ordering.

## Publication rule

The website should default to a selected scenario and make scenario switching prominent. It should not advertise one municipality as the single universal winner. Rank spread and sensitivity should be available as supporting evidence, especially for specialist locations.

The Low-Water AI scenario remains a proxy until the ANA water layer is integrated.
